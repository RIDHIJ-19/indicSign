Tips for the best results

A few small things that make a big difference.

💡

Good lighting

Face a window or lamp so your hand isn't in shadow.

🖼️

Plain background

A simple wall behind you helps your hand stand out.

✋

Stay in frame

Keep your whole hand inside the dashed outline.

⏱️

Hold it steady

Keep each sign still for a second or two before moving on.
